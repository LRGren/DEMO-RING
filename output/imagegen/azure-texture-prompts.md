# Azure beam textures

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Original generated PNGs were copied without image processing into `DEMO RING/Assets/Art/Textures/Spells/Azure/`.

These are black-background grayscale emission textures intended for additive blending. Set their blue/cyan tint in the material. The flow texture is a static image; animation requires scrolling its UV offset. Horizontal tiling was requested during generation but has not been verified in Unity.

## Azure_Beam_Core.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready Unity additive VFX grayscale beam core texture, single flat texture image, wide 4:1 aspect ratio. Primary request: a seamless horizontal luminous energy beam strip mask for a sustained azure magic laser LineRenderer. Black background, grayscale only, no blue tint. U axis left to right runs along beam length and tiles seamlessly, V axis vertical runs across beam width. A clean continuous white narrow central horizontal core occupies 12% of image height, with very smooth symmetric soft gray Gaussian falloff above and below into exact solid black across outer 25% at top and bottom. Uniform brightness along horizontal axis all the way to both left and right edges, no rounded end caps, no taper at left or right, no source flare. Very subtle long parallel wisps hugging center only. Flat unlit emission intensity mask, full bleed UV texture without margins at left/right. No objects, no scene, no perspective, no labels, no text, no checkerboard, no border, no baked colored bloom.

## Azure_Beam_Flow.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production Unity additive VFX grayscale flowing energy beam texture, wide horizontal 4:1 flat UV image. Primary request: a horizontally tileable magic laser energy flow emission mask for the outer layer of a sustained azure beam. Black background, ONLY neutral white and gray. Long thin intertwined flowing plasma filaments, elongated smooth wispy turbulence in horizontal direction, delicate luminous veins meandering about the horizontal midline. White gray narrow irregular ribbons with dark gaps to give motion when the texture scrolls horizontally. Center 45% of image contains the wisps; fade smoothly to exact pure black in the outer 20% along top and bottom edges. Continuous horizontal repeat across left and right edges, both edges have matching brightness and wisp placement. No source orb, no endpoint, no taper or end caps at left/right, no vertical streaks, no stars or scattered particles, no scene, no text, no labels, no frame, no perspective, no checkerboard, no colored tint. Full-bleed single texture asset, not an illustration of a spell in the world.

## Azure_Soft_Glow.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production Unity additive particle VFX radial soft glow sprite, square flat UV texture. Primary request: one centered circular soft light emission mask for the hand source and impact glow of an azure sustained magic beam. Neutral grayscale only, white center and pure black background. Small intensely white round center of radius 6% image width, smooth continuous Gaussian falloff into soft medium gray halo to radius 25%, fading perfectly into pure black before radius 43%. Rotationally symmetric with feathered edges and completely black corners and margins. Very subtle soft radial wisps close to the core, no visible hard ring or sharp disk boundary. A single isolated glow dot, flat emission mask with no perspective. No lens flare, no horizontal streak, no cross, no starburst spikes, no rings, no objects, no scene, no text, no labels, no border, no checkerboard, no colored tint.
